# Feature roadmap
## Done (v1.0)
Core teacher (12 modes) · voice pipeline with confidence checks · deterministic fluency analysis · corrections with verification · mistake dictionary · picture challenges · interview trainer with STAR · vocabulary (SM-2, quizzes, idioms) · 14-topic grammar course · think-in-English drills · listening (US/UK) · pronunciation (minimal pairs, read-aloud, difficult words) · daily adaptive plan · weekly report · assessment/CEFR estimate · reminders (13 categories) · timer & routine · progress dashboard · privacy controls · light/dark themes.

From the “future-ready” list, already included: random speaking topics, story continuation, describe/explain topics, debate with AI, impromptu speaking, public-speaking & presentation modes, idioms, personal mistake dictionary, weekly speaking examination (weekly assessment), emergency quick practice, offline access to cached lessons (grammar course, cached word cards & dialogues).

## Next candidates (only if they don't hurt size, cost or privacy)
1. **Professional email writing & formal/informal rewriting** – text mode reusing the analysis prompt.
2. **Achievement badges** – derived from existing data (streak, words known, sessions).
3. **Personalised roadmap view** – visualise grammar mastery + CEFR targets over weeks.
4. **Monthly fluency assessment report** – compare monthly averages (data already stored).
5. **Pronunciation difficulty tracker** – aggregate read-aloud misrecognised words.
6. **Optional on-device STT (Sherpa-ONNX)** – offline/private mode as a separate download.
7. **Encrypted backup to the user's Google Drive** – manual export already exists.
